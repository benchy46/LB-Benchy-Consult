Reasoning was explicit: differentiation matters more than the general technique, since multiple existing patents already cover "vortex creation" broadly. 2025-04-03. This is a clear example of surfacing an emergent design benefit for the patent claim, not just the original design intent. 2025-08-25. [source](https://lionsbot.slack.com/archives/C07B032TGNM/p1756092697265829)

Hopefully this section is enough." 2025-08-05 to 2025-08-25. [source](https://lionsbot.slack.com/archives/C07B032TGNM/p1756086927080299)
