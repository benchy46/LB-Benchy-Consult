**Purpose:** Architecture-level working channel for the R5 scrubber — cross-subsystem design questions, field/customer-driven change requests, supplier material and MOQ decisions, and failure-mode triage. ## Notable Exchanges

- **The 2026-07-31 water-flow thread is the best single example of him supplying a constraint others hadn't priced in.** Joei Wee was working a dealer request (0.15–0.18 L/m², computed by the team to need ~6,300–7,560 mL/min against a current 500 mL/min max) and had turned to Lucas on pump capability; Benjamin's contribution was neither the pump limit nor the dealer maths but the coupled failure mode — more water makes wheel slip worse. Lucas separately confirmed the pump was never designed past ~1 L/min ("We never needed more than 1L/min cause 60L/h was our target"). The thread is a good illustration of how he participates in a channel he doesn't drive: one sentence, inserted at the point where the group was about to optimise the wrong variable.
- **Corrects himself readily in fast back-and-forth** — "Hmmm that's true my bad", "But idk for sure", "But need to consider sloshing for sure" (all 2026-05-13, within three minutes) — his rapid-fire mode is visibly provisional and self-correcting, quite unlike the settled tone of his `<!channel>` standards posts. Worth noting for register: he does not defend a position he's just been shown is wrong.
- **Terse factual corrections** — "There is no damper. Only spring." (2026-07-13); "Can, just must run with hepa filter." (2026-06-17); "Yes for DV robots" (2026-05-26).

## Recurring Themes / Principles Observed

- **Adjudicator, not driver, in this channel** — unlike `s3-mech-architecture` (where he broadcasts standards), here he mostly lands single-sentence rulings inside other people's threads. His value-add is usually a constraint or a piece of prior test evidence the thread was missing.
- **Names the phase-gate when rejecting scope** — "Such feedback should be given at DV stage not SOP stage" is the same build-phase discipline seen in `r5-ops`/`r5-dv04-changes`, applied to *when a request is allowed to arrive* rather than to what a fix applies to.
- **Closes the supplier avenue before presenting options** — the ball-valve message shows he does the vendor legwork first so the team is choosing among live options, not re-suggesting one he's already killed.
- **Honest about the provenance of his own recall** — "I forgot on what robot, but likely DV" rather than asserting a clean test result. Consistent with his general refusal to manufacture precision he doesn't have.
- **Cost/intervention ordering** — option lists run cheapest-and-least-invasive first (resistor → different supplier → PCB redesign).
