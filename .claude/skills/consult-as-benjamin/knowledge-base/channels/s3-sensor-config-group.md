## Decisions & Reasoning (Benjamin)
Near-empty until September 2026. Through the original sweep his only appearance was the automated "has joined the channel" system message on 2026-05-25; the channel is driven by Vignesh, Darrion Poon, Jack Dyer, Song Shan, WeiTong, Ryan Arya, and Hisham. His first substantive message here lands 2026-09-05:

- **[2026-09-05] Accepted a known sensor-rigidity defect and answered it with process, not hardware.** Ryan Arya opened the thread with a well-formed report: the side depth camera visibly moves with the handlebar, which breaks its calibration — "the motion is not enough to create spurious obstacles, but enough to report inaccurate obstacle height, affecting the Dynamic Posture feature. For instance, 8 cm obstacle can be reported as 11 cm --> too big for pickup, robot doesn't plan towards it." He asked three questions: can the camera be made more rigid, will tightening the bolts be enough, and "In the field, over many cycles of handle up/down, will it work itself loose?" Jack Dyer escalated it to Benjamin as a phase question — "will this be an issue at DV?" — and got the whole reply: "Yup i've noticed that. We will put loctite and torque the screws on DV builds".
  - Three things in one line. He confirms he already knew ("i've noticed that") rather than treating it as news, which is characteristic — a reported problem he is already tracking gets acknowledged, not re-opened. He answers the *phase* question that was actually asked rather than the rigidity question that was asked first. And the fix is threadlocker plus a torque spec — a consumable and a process step, no bracket redesign, no extra fasteners, no mould change.
  - Read this together with the same-problem thread in [#s3-handlebar](./s3-handlebar.md) the previous afternoon (2026-09-04), where Hisham proposed exactly the hardware answer Ryan was fishing for — two more mounting holes per bracket plus brass inserts via a mould mod — and Benjamin refused it on over-constraint grounds ("There would be issues with Double datums so it isn't possible") before landing on the identical Loctite-plus-torque fix. The same conclusion delivered twice in two days to two different audiences: to the mechanical designer with the kinematic reason attached, to the autonomy engineer as a bare statement of what will happen at DV.
  - Note what he does *not* answer: Ryan's field-durability question ("over many cycles of handle up/down, will it work itself loose?"). The Loctite answer is arguably the implicit reply, but the long-term service question is left open, and the fix is scoped explicitly to "DV builds". — [source](https://lionsbot.slack.com/archives/C0AC2NB749X/p1788613237576919?thread_ts=1788507154.521409&cid=C0AC2NB749X)
