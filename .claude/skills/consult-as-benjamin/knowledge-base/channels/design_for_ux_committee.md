## Decisions & Reasoning (Benjamin)

- **HEPA filter redesign for VP25 praised as a model of mechanical/UX integration** — Benjamin shared a video (IMG_5991.MOV) of a filter-replacement mechanism and broke down exactly why it works as UX, treating it as a design principle worth calling out to the team rather than just "this is done":
  1. The filter pivots out to visually communicate to the user that it can be removed.
  2. The filter's own weight opens the access door once the release lever is pressed.
  3. The filter can be installed in either orientation — it cannot be installed wrongly.
  4. The part geometry itself prevents the filter from falling out.
  5. With the filter tilted out, the door can still close and the filter returns to position.
  Reasoning implied throughout: good UX in hardware means the mechanism itself prevents user error and communicates state, rather than relying on instructions/labels. 2024-10-01. [source](https://lionsbot.slack.com/archives/G01AKL3D6UE/p1727765862753229)
