# #robotic-cleaning-tech-show-info
**Slack channel ID:** C0AQQ7CUL6L
**Purpose:** A field-notes dump channel for a specific cleaning-robotics trade show (China Clean 2026, late March/early April 2026). Used almost exclusively by Benjamin to log rapid-fire competitive teardown observations — photos and videos of competitor robots' internals, docking stations, cleaning mechanisms — as he walked the show floor.

All in-range activity in this channel falls in a single 2-day window: 2026-03-31 to 2026-04-01. There is no earlier or later activity (the channel appears created for this specific show).

## Recurring Themes / Principles Observed
- Consistent focus on **serviceability/UX of maintenance** (how hard is it to change a brush, a squeegee, empty a tank) as a first-class evaluation criterion, not just cleaning performance.
- Recurring skepticism toward marketing claims not yet proven in practice (squeegee self-cleaning, AI spot-cleaning "under ideal conditions").
- Attention to mechanical detail: linkages, bumper sensors, castor placement, lidar count/placement — suggests his evaluation lens is mechanical/electrical engineering first.
