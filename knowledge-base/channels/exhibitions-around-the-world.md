# #exhibitions-around-the-world
**Slack channel ID:** C050JLZDG9H
**Purpose:** A shared, ongoing team channel (much larger cast than the show-specific channel) where LionsBot staff — mainly Dylan Ng, Wei Hua, Seah Tat Leong, Fu Hui, Sunardi Tay, Daniel Tan, Vicky Wang, and occasionally Benjamin — post competitive intelligence and supplier discoveries from trade shows worldwide (China Clean, Interclean Amsterdam, ISSA North America, etc.) over roughly two years.

Full history in range covers 2024-08-06 through 2026-04-18 (confirmed no messages before or after that window). The overwhelming majority of messages are from other team members (especially Dylan Ng and Wei Hua) posting competitor findings; Benjamin's own posts are a small fraction of total volume, concentrated around the China Clean 2026 show (late March/early April 2026).

## Recurring Themes / Principles Observed
- Consistent with the show-info channel: strong interest in manufacturing/serviceability details (motor sourcing, PCB waterproofing methods, squeegee cartridge replacement UX) over marketing/spec-sheet claims.
- Willingness to directly assign follow-up ("please more details," tagging specific colleagues) is common channel-wide (mostly from Dylan Ng), and Benjamin participates in that same tag-and-delegate pattern rather than writing long directives himself.
- Note: most of the deep technical/competitive analysis in this channel (Gausium, Pudu, Keenon, Tennant, Hako, etc. teardown threads) comes from Dylan Ng, Wei Hua, and Seah Tat Leong, not Benjamin — care was taken not to attribute their analysis to him.
