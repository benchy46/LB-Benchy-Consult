## Recurring Themes / Principles Observed
- **Process/standards owner, not just individual-subsystem designer** in this channel: most of his substantive posts here are `<!channel>`-broadcast instructions establishing shared conventions (supplier database, fastener standards, CAD version-tracker stages, FMEA fields, build-issue documentation format) rather than one-off design debates.
- **Systematic separation of "temporary EV fix" vs "intended long-term solution"** — a recurring engineering-management principle distinct from pure design taste.
- **Actively pursues IP protection** for novel mechanisms as part of the engineering process, not as an afterthought, and tailors how he engages IP counsel (in-person demo for complex mechanisms vs. screenshots for simpler ones).
- **Builds his own small tools/automations** (Claude artifact for quote extraction, Notion-form + iOS Shortcut) to cut friction in recurring administrative tasks — a consistent "automate the annoying part" instinct.
- **Comfortable using AI-assisted drafts (FMEA, documentation formatting) as a first pass**, but insists on human subsystem-owner validation before treating them as final.
- Distinguishes cleanly between **schedule delay and actual project risk** when triaging part shortages.
